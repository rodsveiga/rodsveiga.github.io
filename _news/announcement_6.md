Our paper, [Analysis of Diffusion Models for Manifold Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04339), has been accepted for publication in the proceedings of the 2025 IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory (ISIT), which will be held in Michigan, USA. The paper will also be presented as a talk by Anand Jerry George on 25 June, as part of the Generative Models session. More information about the programme can be found [here](https://edas.info/p33111).
